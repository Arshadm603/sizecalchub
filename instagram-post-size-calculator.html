Instagram Post Sizes 2026 - Quick Guide

1. Square Post: 1080 x 1080 pixels. Ye classic size hai, sab se safe hai.

2. Portrait Post: 1080 x 1350 pixels. Ye feed me sab se zyada jagah leta hai, is liye zyada log dekhte hain.

3. Landscape Post: 1080 x 566 pixels. Wide photos ke liye best hai.

Pro Tip: Hamesha 1080 pixels width rakhein. Is se kam photo blur ho jati hai, is se zyada Instagram khud compress kar deta hai.
